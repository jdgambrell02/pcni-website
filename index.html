Our mission

Pass Christian Neighbor Initiative (PCNI) is a local, community-led effort to meet urgent needs and build long-term opportunity.

We focus on safe housing, essential repairs, and pathways to opportunity, delivered with dignity and in partnership with neighbors, churches, and civic groups across the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

We meet neighbors where they are, and we help them stay in the homes and community they love.

How we help

Our programs & services

Storm resilience

Fortified Roof Assistance

PCNI works directly within Pass Christian neighborhoods to help eligible homeowners navigate and afford the installation of IBHS FORTIFIED Roof™ systems. We assist residents with state and federal mitigation grant applications, contractor coordination, and financial support for costs not covered by grant programs or insurance discounts, strengthening homes against future storms and reducing long-term recovery costs for the community.

Everyday stability

Minor Home & Automotive Repair

PCNI assists neighbors with essential small repairs, both around the home and for personal vehicles, so residents can stay safe, mobile, and self-sufficient. This includes basic household fixes, simple electrical or plumbing problems, minor exterior repairs, battery replacements, tire assistance, and other quick fixes that can make a big difference.

Tax Deductibility Notice

The Pass Christian Neighborhood Initiative (PCNI) is a local, community-led initiative under The Gulf Coast Community Foundation (GCCF), a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. For any contributions made in connection with a fundraising event, ticket purchase, raffle, or merchandise sale in which goods or services are provided, the tax-deductible portion of the contribution is limited to the amount paid in excess of the fair market value of the goods or services received. Donors are responsible for determining the tax-deductible amount of their contributions in consultation with their tax advisor.
