PCNI Announces $139,000 in 2026 Community Support

Pass Christian, Mississippi — August 11, 2026

PCNI is proud to announce that, in 2026, we have secured $139,000 in grants and in-kind donations to help Pass Christian homeowners strengthen their homes, improve safety, and remain independent in their community.

Through the Gulf Coast Community Foundation (GCCF), PCNI has been awarded $127,000 in funding to support our work helping eligible homeowners install IBHS FORTIFIED Roof™ systems.

This funding is made possible in part through the Federal Home Loan Bank of Dallas (FHLB Dallas) FORTIFIED Fund – Homeownership, which provides grants to eligible homeowners for the installation of FORTIFIED roofs. PCNI works with homeowners and community partners to help navigate available funding, coordinate the installation process, and ensure that families who need assistance have access to these important mitigation resources.

Additional 2026 Support

  • $11,000 in in-kind donations supporting FORTIFIED Roof installations.
  • $10,000 in donations supporting essential home repairs for neighbors in need.

Building a Stronger, More Resilient Pass Christian

These resources allow PCNI to do more than simply respond after a storm. We are helping our neighbors prepare before the next storm arrives.

A FORTIFIED Roof is designed to provide greater protection against severe weather and can significantly reduce the potential for storm-related damage. By helping homeowners strengthen their homes today, PCNI is investing in safer families, more resilient neighborhoods, and a stronger Pass Christian community for tomorrow.

$139,000 committed in 2026 to help Pass Christian neighbors.

PCNI is grateful to the Gulf Coast Community Foundation, the Federal Home Loan Bank of Dallas, our contractors, donors, and community partners whose support makes this work possible.
